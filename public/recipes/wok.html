Wok

Soy-marinated chicken and veggies tossed with rice, finished with sriracha, fried onions and spring onion.

Serves 2–3

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Bring water with salt to a boil.
  2. Put the rice in.
  3. Cut the chicken and let it sit in some soy sauce and chopped garlic.
  4. Fry the chicken in the wok until barely done.
  5. Set the chicken aside.
  6. Put extra oil in the pan.
  7. Add the veggies.
  8. Stir-fry for about 5 minutes (until the pan is dry).
  9. Add some soy sauce.
  10. Add the chicken back in.
  11. Stir-fry for another 2 minutes.
  12. Add the rice.
  13. Stir.
  14. Add sriracha.
  15. Serve, garnished with fried onions and spring onion.
