Butter chicken

Yoghurt-marinated chicken in a rich tomato, butter and cream sauce, served with rice.

Serves 3–4

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Cut everything that needs cutting.
  2. Put the chicken in a bowl:
    1. Add salt.
    2. Add garam masala.
    3. Add smoked paprika.
    4. Add 2 cloves of finely chopped garlic.
    5. Add half of the ginger.
    6. Add 4 big tablespoons of Greek yoghurt.
    7. Stir thoroughly.
  3. Put a pan on medium-high heat:
    1. Add about ¼ of the butter.
    2. Add the chicken.
    3. Cook until just done and slightly browned.
  4. Take the chicken out of the pan and put it in a bowl.
  5. Start cooking the rice.
  6. Put a big cube of butter (the rest of it) in the pan:
    1. Add the onion, the rest of the ginger, the rest of the garlic and the chilli flakes.
    2. Add salt.
    3. Add garam masala.
    4. Add smoked paprika.
    5. Let it cook until soft.
    6. Add the can of diced tomatoes.
    7. Stir and let it cook until the sauce has thickened.
    8. Add the 20 cl cream.
    9. Stir.
    10. Optionally, blend the sauce a bit.
    11. Add the chicken.
    12. Let it simmer for 5 minutes.
    13. Add lime juice to taste.
  7. Serve with the rice, topped with coriander and/or spring onion, as in the picture :)
