Curry Andreas

A vegetarian coconut curry, all in one wok. Serve with rice.

30 min

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Cut the veggies.
  2. Heat oil in a big pan (e.g. a wok).
  3. Add the garlic and red curry paste.
  4. Before the garlic burns, add the coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce and sugar.
  5. Add the sweet potatoes.
  6. Start boiling water for the rice.
  7. Once the sweet potatoes are getting soft, add the broccoli.
  8. Start cooking the rice.
  9. Once the broccoli is soft, add the chickpeas.
  10. Taste and season with soy sauce, salt and sugar.
  11. Add a splash of lime.
  12. Serve with rice.
