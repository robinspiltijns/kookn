Beef fried rice

Crispy fried rice with seared beef, topped with a sunny-side-up egg. Uses up old rice.

Serves 1

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Salt the beef.
  2. Let the pan heat up.
  3. Put oil in the pan.
  4. Put in the beef.
  5. Sear the beef and let it cook for 1–3 minutes, depending on preference.
  6. If you're adding veggies, add them now and cook until almost done.
  7. Turn down the heat.
  8. Add the garlic and shallot.
  9. Stir for 1–2 minutes.
  10. Add some of the eggs (the others can be served on top).
  11. Let it sit until the eggs are halfway done.
  12. Add the rice to the pan and stir.
  13. Add the sauces: soy sauce, oyster sauce, rice vinegar and sesame oil.
  14. Stir it up a bit.
  15. Press the rice flat in the pan and let it sit for a while (trying to make it crispy).
  16. Serve with a sunny-side-up egg and spring onion on top.
