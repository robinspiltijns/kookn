Quick weeknight gnocchi

Spicy sausage, green veg and gnocchi, made creamy with parmigiano and pasta water. One pan, one pot.

Serves 2–3 · 15–20 min

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Cut the veggies.
  2. Cut the garlic.
  3. Remove the skin from the sausage and cut it into gnocchi-sized chunks.
  4. Start boiling a small pot of water and salt it.
  5. Heat a pan and add oil.
  6. Add the sausage to the pan.
  7. Once the sausage starts to brown, add the garlic and pili pili.
  8. After about 1–2 minutes (when the sausage is basically cooked through): add the veggies, turn the heat down to medium and put on a lid.
  9. Stir every now and then. Put the lid on or off to manage the moisture in the pan: it should not be too wet.
  10. When the veggies have a nice texture, add the gnocchi to the boiling water.
  11. When the gnocchi are done (floating), add them to the pan along with a generous amount of pasta water.
  12. Turn off the heat, let it cool a bit and add the parm.
  13. Mix, adding parm and pasta water until you get the creaminess you want.
  14. Serve with some more parm on top.
