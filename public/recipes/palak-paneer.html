Palak paneer

Browned paneer in a spiced, creamy spinach sauce. Serve with rice and/or garlic naan.

Serves 3–4 · 30–40 min

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Start bringing salted water to a boil.
  2. Wash the spinach.
  3. Chop the paneer into cubes.
  4. If you have a mixer/blender, roughly chop the following; otherwise, chop them finely:
    1. garlic
    2. ginger
    3. tomatoes
    4. onion
    5. chilli
  5. Once the water is boiling, boil the spinach for 2 minutes, then immediately cool it under cold running water (blanching).
  6. Heat some oil in a pan on high heat and add the paneer.
  7. Season the paneer generously with salt.
  8. Blast the paneer at max heat until it is nicely browned.
  9. In a new pot, heat some oil and add the ginger, garlic, onion and a good amount of turmeric and salt.
  10. Once everything has sweated down, add the tomatoes and chilli (and chilli flakes, if using).
  11. This is a good time to start prepping the rice/naan.
  12. Once everything is soft, add garam masala, ground cumin, coriander and salt to taste. Go for a strong flavour.
  13. Let it sit for 1–2 minutes, then add the spinach to the pot.
  14. Stir a bit.
  15. If possible, use a mixer to get the texture you want; otherwise, stir until you get a nice consistency.
  16. Salt if needed.
  17. Add the cream and paneer.
  18. Add salt and spices to taste.
  19. Cover and let simmer on low heat for about 2 minutes.
  20. Add lime juice to taste.
  21. Drizzle some cream over the sauce for presentation.
  22. Serve.
