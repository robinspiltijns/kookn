Pizza

Neapolitan-style pizza from a 450 °C oven: dough, sauce, baking, and a dozen topping combinations.

Dough · Sauce · Baking · Toppings

Dough

Ingredients (4 pizzas)

Process

  1. Put the flour in a big bowl.
  2. Boil ⅓ of the water.
  3. Keep the rest of the water at room temperature.
  4. Mix the hot and cold water, add the salt and yeast, and stir until there are no clumps.
  5. Add the water to the flour gradually and knead until firm and stretchy (20 min+).
  6. Make the dough into a nice ball.
  7. Let it rest for 2 hours.
  8. Divide into smaller balls of 200 g.
  9. Let them rest again for 30 minutes.

Sauce

Ingredients

Process

  1. Chop the garlic.
  2. Add the tomatoes, garlic and salt to a bowl.
  3. Crush with your hands.

Baking

  1. Start the oven.
  2. Get a bowl of semolina.
  3. Once the dough has rested 30 minutes and the oven is at 450 °C, take a ball.
  4. Put the ball in the semolina (top down).
  5. Start rounding:
    1. Push out from the middle to the edge.
    2. Pick up the dough → steering wheel OR flip that sh*t (see video).
  6. Sauce it.
  7. Cheese it.
  8. In the oven.
  9. After 30 s–1 min, turn it around, then another 30 s–1 min.
  10. Garnish.
  11. Olive oil.
