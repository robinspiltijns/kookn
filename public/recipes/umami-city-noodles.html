Umami city noodles

Mince and noodles in a peanut butter, soy and crispy chilli oil sauce. One wok.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Wok on, heat on, oil in.
  2. Add the mince and salt.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the noodles according to the package.
  4. Roughly chop the garlic.
  5. Once the mince starts browning, but isn't fully done, add the veggies and garlic.
  6. Stir once in a while until the veggies are almost done.
  7. Deglaze with rice vinegar.
  8. Let it cook until the sharpness of the rice vinegar is gone.
  9. Add soy sauce, peanut butter and crispy chilli oil until you get a strong flavour and a good balance.
    1. For 3–4 people: ~2 tablespoons of peanut butter, 2 big teaspoons of chilli oil, and soy sauce until salty enough. You should taste the peanut butter.
  10. Add the noodles and mix.
  11. Taste, and add soy sauce and chilli oil to taste (not more peanut butter: at this point it will clump).
  12. Add lime juice.
  13. Serve with spring onion on top.
