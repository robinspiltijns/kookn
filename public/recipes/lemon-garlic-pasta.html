Pasta al limone

Garlic, chilli and lemon in olive oil and butter, finished with parmigiano and parsley.

Ingredients

Rule of thumb: ⅓ lemon per 250 g pasta.

Steps

  1. Optional: watch the video to warm the heart.
  2. Cut the garlic and chilli.
  3. Zest the lemon.
  4. Boil the pasta.
  5. Put olive oil in a pan and add the chilli, garlic and most of the zest.
  6. Add the pasta to the pan.
  7. Add butter.
  8. Stir.
  9. Add lemon juice.
  10. Add parsley.
  11. Add the rest of the lemon zest.
  12. Add parm.
  13. Add pasta water.
  14. Taste.
