One-pot sausage & spinach pasta

Sausage (or mushrooms), spinach and pasta, all made in the same pot and turned creamy with parmigiano.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Cut the mushrooms, or the sausage without its skin.
  2. Wash the spinach.
  3. In a pot, add olive oil, the meat/mushrooms, pepper and salt, and turn on the heat.
  4. Slice the garlic.
  5. Once the meat is done, set it aside.
  6. In the same pot, add the spinach, some oil, the garlic, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  7. Once done, set the spinach aside.
  8. In the same pot, add the pasta and just enough water to cover it.
  9. Bring to a boil and stir occasionally.
  10. Meanwhile, grate the parm.
  11. Once the pasta is done, you want a bit of pasta water left in the pot. If there's too much, take some out (keep it aside, just in case).
  12. Add ¾ of the parm to the pot and stir to make it creamy. Add water back if it's too dry.
  13. Add back the meat and spinach, and the ricotta if using.
  14. Stir.
  15. Add a squeeze of lemon juice.
  16. Serve with the rest of the parm and olive oil on top.

Source

Ethan's 15-minute meals on Pro Home Cooks
