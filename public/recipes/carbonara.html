Pasta alla carbonara

The way Antonio intended.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Optional: watch the video to warm the heart.
  2. Put some water on to boil and add a generous amount of salt.
  3. Grate the pecorino.
  4. Add 3 yolks and 3 whole eggs to the grated pecorino. (A lot has already been said about the right ratio of yolks to whole eggs. Figure out what you like.)
  5. Add some black pepper to the mixture.
  6. Once the water boils, add the pasta.
  7. Put a pan on high heat and add the bacon.
  8. Once the pasta is done, add it to the bacon. Keep a cup of the pasta water to the side.
  9. Mix the pasta and bacon until it has cooled down a bit.
  10. Add the cheese mixture to the pan, with a bit of the pasta water.
  11. Toss and mix. If the sauce is too thick, add more pasta water and keep mixing.
  12. Top off with a generous amount of black pepper.
  13. Serve.

Scales up nicely for a crowd.
