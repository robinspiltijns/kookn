Pasta all’amatriciana

Crispy bacon or guanciale, a smooth tomato sauce and plenty of pecorino.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes.
  2. Put the water on to boil.
  3. Fry the bacon.
  4. Add a bit of pepper to the bacon.
  5. Once the water boils, put the pasta in the water and the tomatoes in the pan with the bacon.
  6. Add pepper to the tomato sauce.
  7. Let the tomato sauce cook down well.
  8. When the pasta is almost done, add it to the pan.
  9. Add pasta water and pecorino until the texture and taste are right.
  10. Season with pepper and salt.
  11. Serve.

Variations

  • Fry bell pepper hard and add it to the sauce.
  • Use mascarpone, parmigiano and basil instead of pecorino.
