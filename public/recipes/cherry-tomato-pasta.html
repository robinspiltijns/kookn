Cherry tomato pasta

Cherry tomatoes cooked down with onion and garlic, made creamy with parmigiano and pasta water.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Dice the onion.
  2. Slice the garlic.
  3. Start boiling water with salt.
  4. Put a big pan on the heat and add olive oil and the onion.
  5. Once soft, add the garlic and salt.
  6. 30 seconds later, add the cherry tomatoes, black pepper and salt.
  7. Boil the pasta.
  8. Once the cherry tomatoes start to soften, squish them.
  9. Cut the parsley.
  10. Let the cherry tomatoes reduce until the pasta is done.
  11. Add the pasta to the cherry tomatoes (keep the pasta water).
  12. Add pasta water, parm, salt and black pepper until the taste and texture are nice.
  13. Add the parsley.
  14. Serve with parm.
