Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. One pan, 30 minutes.

Serves 2 · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper until soft, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika. Stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season, and simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
  4. Make 4 wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook 5–7 minutes, until the whites are set.
  5. Top with parsley and serve with bread.
Tips & variations
  • Add crumbled feta in the last 2 minutes.
  • For runny yolks, take it off the heat as soon as the whites turn opaque.
