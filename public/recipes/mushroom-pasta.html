Creamy mushroom pasta

Browned mushrooms, white wine and cream, finished with lemon, parmigiano and parsley.

Ingredients

Steps

  1. Put salted water on to boil for the pasta.
  2. Cut the mushrooms into chunky pieces.
  3. Heat olive oil (and/or butter) in a big pan. Add the mushrooms and salt.
  4. While the mushrooms cook, slice the onion into slivers and slice the garlic.
  5. Try to brown the mushrooms, until the water from the mushrooms has evaporated.
  6. Add a bit more oil and the onion.
  7. Once the onion has softened, add the garlic and a few cracks of black pepper. Let it cook for 2 minutes; roughly chop the parsley in the meantime.
  8. Add the white wine and let it reduce more or less completely.
  9. Add the cream, nutmeg and pepper.
  10. Add the pasta to the boiling water.
  11. 1–2 minutes before the pasta is done, turn the heat off under the sauce.
  12. Add lemon juice.
  13. Add the pasta to the pan (keep the pasta water).
  14. Add parm and pasta water until the taste and texture are good.
  15. Add the parsley.
  16. Serve.
